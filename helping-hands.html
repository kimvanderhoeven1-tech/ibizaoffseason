Helping Hands

The people who keep island life running in winter.

Winter on Ibiza is easy right up to the moment you need someone: a nanny for Saturday night, a chef for the birthday you're hosting, somebody to look after the house while you're in Amsterdam, two extra pairs of hands for the boat or the party. These are people we know or have been pointed to by people we trust — small, local, reachable. The list grows as the season goes on. Offer a service? Send it in; it goes on here and in the weekly once we've had a look.

Away this winter? If your house stands empty between visits, start with Mayte under House & property. Know a good nanny, chef or fixer? Tell us — a name and a number is enough.
