Boots on, the island's yours

Hola,

November is hiking month here: cool enough to climb, warm enough to swim at the bottom, and nobody on the path. This weekend you can walk for the lizards at Ses Salines, dance in a restaurant, hear choirs in a village church and wave Sinterklaas off to Holland. Next week: herbs, an Italian long table and the first winter party.

Tonight
Chimenea opens at Las Dalias Café, Sant Carles · 20:00
First night of Las Dalias' new Friday: dinner and dancing around the fireplace in the café, with La Tipi's Words of Niō and Alex Minschka playing. Free entry. Details →
Friday cinema: A Real Pain, Can Jeroni, Sant Josep · 20:00 ★ Local
Jesse Eisenberg's film in original version with Spanish subtitles. Free. Details →
Also: steak night at Higo in Santa Eulària, salsa and bachata at Zazú's Latin Club in Platja d'en Bossa, and independent cinema in original version at Cine Regio in Sant Antoni, 20:30.
Saturday
Dalt Vila's landmark buildings, guided heritage walk · 10:30
Two hours on the civil, military and religious architecture of the old town, starting at Plaça de la Catedral. Free; register at madinayabisa@eivissa.es. Details →
Co-Creating Culture opens at Can Cristòfol · 18:00
Opening night of the exhibition curated by Roseline de Thélin at the finca on the Sant Carles road, until 22:00. Free, and the first of three November gatherings there. Details →
Laundry Room at Pacha Restaurant · 22:30
Pacha's winter club night in the restaurant, until 05:00: Angel Linde, Nicolau, Nima Gorji, Stephane, Tizzio b2b Koke. Free for Ibiza residents and with a dinner reservation; otherwise tickets at the door. Details →
Timemachine, vinyl only at Es Birra · 23:00
The Ricardistas (AKA Jay, Donna Sami and 4Pleasure) play records until late in Ibiza Town. Free. Details →
Also: Sant Carles Xtrem obstacle race at the Las Dalias car park in the afternoon (kids free), Supernova at Las Dalias from 20:00 (café free until 23:00), and paint-your-own ceramics at Studiø Raffo in Cala Llonga, 14:00–17:00 (€79, book ahead).
Sunday
Ses Salines lizard walk · 10:00
Ibiza Outdoors walks for Ibiza & Formentera Preservation: along the beach to the pirate tower and back through the forest where the wall lizards live. 7.5 km, three hours, mostly flat. Free to join, donate on the day. Details →
Sinterklaas send-off at Higo, Santa Eulària · from 13:00
Sinterklaas leaves for the Netherlands and the island's Dutch families wave him off: an activity trail through town for the kids from 13:00, then the party at Higo with food, an ice cream and a surprise present. Kids €25 with the trail, €15 without; adults €25 with paella or satay and a drink. Bring a small unwrapped gift for island children who would otherwise go without. Tickets →
Choirs in the church of Sant Josep · 12:00 ★ Local
Three island choirs in the village church, then vermouth on the square. Free. Details →
Nido, The Sunday Edit at Las Dalias · 12:00
Garden session from noon, free entry, with Pomboklap, Cris 44, Ares, Mika Somoza and Shadeh & Sena. From 20:00 it moves inside to Akasha; residents free until 21:00. Details →
Anna-B & Ainvar live at Can Cosmi, Santa Agnès · 20:00 ★ Local
Pop-rock from the 90s and 00s on the terrace of the village bar opposite the church. Free. Details →
Also: Sunday roast at La Chickeria and at Can Mimosa, both in Santa Gertrudis, 13:00–17:00, and the Sant Joan market until 16:00.
Next week

Monday 9 is stamppot night at Higo: kale mash with fresh sausage, from 18:00, also to take away. Tuesday 10 Cynthia van Winden starts a new morning in Sant Carles: group workout at 09:30, then breakfast on the terrace of La Cantina.

Wednesday 11 Ibiza Outdoors walks to Es Portitxol, the hidden bay near Sant Miquel (8 km, medium-hard, donation). Thursday 12 Barbara and Etienne set the long table at The Food Atelier by Bartienne for an Italian dinner night (19:30, €100, reserve on 608 792 705).

Friday 13 Pocomucho opens its winter season at Paradise Lost in Ibiza Town, 19:30–03:00, no VIP and no guestlist. Saturday 14: a day of talks on culture, land and art at Can Cristòfol (book via WhatsApp Bex, +34 638 497 901) and the northern choirs in the church of Sant Joan at 19:00.
