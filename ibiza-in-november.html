Ibiza in November

The island exhales

NovemberDecemberJanuaryFebruaryMarchApril

November is the month the island gets back to itself. The last hippy market packs up, the clubs are dark, and what's left is 19-degree afternoons, a sea still warm enough to swim in, village fiestas with free food, and tables at restaurants you couldn't get near in August.

This guide is written by people who live here year-round. Everything below links to the live agenda, the Open in Winter list and the retreats calendar, which are updated every week — so the dates on this page are current, not last year's.

Weather in Ibiza in November

Mild, mostly sunny, with the year's first proper rain. Pack for spring in northern Europe: a jumper for the evenings, a jacket for the one wet day a week, and swimwear — locals swim all month.

The first half of the month is usually the kinder one — a run of 22-degree days is normal — and the first northern tramuntana blows through around the middle. When it does, the island's beaches guide tells you which cove is sheltered from which wind.

What makes November special

Tots Sants and bunyols (1 November)

All Saints' Day is a holiday. Bakeries and village squares fry bunyols — sugar-dusted pumpkin doughnuts — and families visit the cemeteries, which are full of flowers. Sant Rafel holds a children's fiesta with a trencada (a nut-cracking game), and Ibiza Town opens its old cemetery for a theatrical night visit.

The patron-saint fiestas: Sant Carles (4 Nov) and Santa Gertrudis (16 Nov)

Two of the island's best village fiestas fall in November. Mass, ball pagès (the island's folk dance, in full costume), a brass band, and then free wine and food in the square. Nobody minds you joining. Sant Carles is the one with Las Dalias next door; Santa Gertrudis has the island's most cosmopolitan village square and a Podenc Race to open its fiesta month on the 1st.

Las Dalias switches to winter mode

The famous hippy market runs on Saturdays through November and December, then closes for January. The Punta Arabí market in Es Canar has its last Wednesday at the end of October — November is when the island's market life moves to the small ones: Forada, Sant Joan, the Friday farmers' market in Sant Antoni.

Choir season

Every November the island's choirs tour the parish churches — Sant Josep (8 Nov), Sant Joan (14 Nov), closing in the Dalt Vila convent (15 Nov). Free, candlelit, and about as un-touristic as Ibiza gets.

The first Christmas lights

Ibiza Town switches on its lights and opens the Christmas market on Vara de Rey in the last days of the month. Until then the almond trees are bare and the island is at its quietest; by 1 December it is in Christmas mode.

Matances

Pig-slaughter season starts in the countryside — the sobrassada you eat in spring is made now. A few fincas open the day to visitors; see the Winter Table for who, and ask us for a table.

Ten things to do in Ibiza in November

1

Walk to Ses Portes del Cel

The cliff path above Santa Agnès, 8 km, with the island's biggest view at the end. Do it in November light, around four o'clock, and finish with a drink in the village.

2

Join the Sant Carles fiesta

4 November, from midday: mass, ball pagès, band and a free lunch in the square. Then Las Dalias café for the afternoon.

3

Do the Saturday market circuit

Forada first (the farmers, the best bread), then Las Dalias, then Juntos Farm for lunch. All three run every Saturday in November.

4

Swim — yes, really

The sea is 20°C, warmer than the North Sea in August. Es Cavallet with lunch at La Escollera (open all year, log fire for grey days) is the November classic.

5

Take the free heritage walk through Dalt Vila

7 November, 10:30, from the cathedral square — the council's own guides, in the old town without the crowds. Later in the month: dramatised night tours.

6

Book a retreat

November is the island's second retreat season: women's circles, yoga intensives, breathwork and sound healing, mostly in fincas in the north. Eight on the calendar this month, from €300.

7

Go to a village cinema night

Can Jeroni in Sant Josep shows films in the original language on Fridays, free. Teatro España in Santa Eulària runs Thursday to Sunday.

8

Find live music in a finca

When the clubs close, the small stages open: blues at Can Jordi on Thursdays and Saturdays, Thursday nights at Cas Costas, vinyl by the fireplace at Las Dalias Café.

9

Spa, sauna, sea

The rural hotels' spas stay open and quiet; Es Cucons until 15 November, Can Curreu and Can Lluc all winter. Pair with a cold swim and you've done the island's winter ritual.

10

Start Spanish

The Santa Eulària council's free classes for EU residents run Monday and Wednesday from October; private tutors and the language school take beginners any week. The best November investment there is.

What's open in Ibiza in November

More than people think. The club-strip resorts close; the villages don't. Santa Gertrudis, Sant Josep, Santa Eulària and Ibiza Town are fully open; Sant Carles, Sant Joan, Sant Mateu and Santa Agnès keep their village bars and one or two restaurants each. On the beaches, La Escollera (Es Cavallet), Yemanjá (Cala Jondal) and Sa Caleta are open all winter; Hostal La Torre reopens on 17 November for tapas and the sunset. The rural hotels with spas — Es Cucons, Can Curreu, Can Lluc — stay open, Es Cucons until mid-month.

The full list, village by village with opening days, is Open in Winter. It's the question we get asked most, so it's the page we update most.

Ibiza in November — quick answers

Is Ibiza worth visiting in November?

Yes, if you want the island rather than the party: walking weather, a warm sea, open villages, fiestas and restaurants without a wait. No, if you want clubs — the last big closings are in October and the superclubs stay shut until May.

Is everything closed in Ibiza in November?

No. The resort strips (Platja d'en Bossa, parts of Sant Antoni bay, Es Canar) largely close; Ibiza Town and the villages stay open year-round. Our Open in Winter list has around 60 places with their winter days.

Can you swim in Ibiza in November?

The sea is about 20°C in early November and 18°C by the end — colder than August, warmer than most of Europe's summer. Locals swim all month; pick a cove sheltered from the day's wind.

Does it rain a lot in Ibiza in November?

November is the wettest month, but that means around 50–60 mm over five or six days, usually short, heavy showers rather than grey weeks. Most days are sunny.

What's the best thing to do in Ibiza in November?

Our vote: a village fiesta (Sant Carles on the 4th or Santa Gertrudis on the 16th), a long cliff walk, and a Saturday on the market circuit. All free.
