Open in Winter

"What's still open in winter?"

It's the question everyone asks from November on: where can you still eat, drink, dance and sit in the sun? Here's our answer — the restaurants, bars, fincas, markets, galleries and little Spanish village spots that keep going all winter, with a terrace in the sun, a fondue, a concert, an opening or a sunset. Winter is the art season here — the galleries that close in August are the ones that open in November; tap Art to see them. Local is reserved for the Spanish- and Ibicenco-run village places — the bar on the square, the café with one dish, the beach restaurant that's been there since the seventies — the things no guide lists. Tap Breakfast for where the island starts its day. Grouped by village, so you know where to end a walk.

Something closed for the winter, or just reopened? Tell us — we check and fix it the same day.

Es Cucons

Eat · Stay · Wellness · Music

Rural hotel in a 17th-century finca on the Pla de Corona, with a spa, sauna, hot tub, restaurant and live concerts. Closes mid-November and reopens at the end of February, just as the almond blossom fades — the Santa Agnès address for a late-winter weekend.

Open until 15 Nov · reopens end of February

Las Puertas del Cielo

Eat · Drink · Sunset

Clifftop bar-restaurant above the north-west coast, named for the 'gates of heaven' view. One of the island's great sunset spots and the natural end to the Ses Balandres walk. Closes 15 November, back at the end of February.

Winter hours 10–17 & 19–23 · Tuesdays closed

Can Cosmi

Drink · Eat · Local · Music

The village bar-restaurant opposite the church: the island's most talked-about tortilla, lamb chops, rustic salads and a terrace in the sun. Salsa nights in winter — ask at the bar for the next one.

Open in winter

Sa Palmera

Eat

Classic Spanish cooking on the village square — the locals' choice after a walk. Simple, honest plates and a terrace that catches the winter sun.

Bodega Sa Cova

Wine · Workshop

Family winery in the Sant Mateu valley, the island's wine country: a walk through the vines and the cellar, then a tasting paired with island cheeses and sobrassada. Combine with lunch at Ses Casetes on the square.

Bodegas Can Maymó

Wine

Small Sant Mateu bodega between the almond trees — reds, a rosé and a sweet wine sold straight from the finca. Buy a bottle, drink it on the terrace at sunset over the vineyards. In early December the whole valley celebrates the Festa del Vi Pagès with the new wine.

Tasting room seasonal — 'see you next season'; wines in shops and restaurants all winter

Ojo de Ibiza

Wine

Dieter Meier's organic winery on twelve hectares of old terraces on the Pla de Sant Mateu, planted in 2015: the island's most polished bottles, made where the almond trees bloom in February. The tasting room by the vines closes for winter, but the wines are on the island's best lists and in SOMM and Vino&Co — drink them while you wait for spring.

Es Pins Cala Pada

Breakfast · Eat · Music · Local

Since 1976, hidden among the pines above Cala Pada on the Es Canar road (km 3.1): island flavours from a wood-fired kitchen, breakfasts, long lunches and dinners under the trees, open from eight in the morning until two at night, every day of the winter. Café-restaurant with its own little bakery at the top of the village: Parisian croissants and cinnamon rolls, sourdough, poke and big salads, cakes that change daily. Can Mimosa

Eat · Drink

A 200-year-old farmhouse tucked behind the square, run by Holly and Pedro: farmhouse cooking from island produce, a fireplace-and-garden kind of place, and the Sunday roast that half the island's expats plan their week around from October to May. They host private birthdays, anniversaries and office dinners of any size, tailored — email holly@canmimosaibiza.com.

Swedish sommelier Anders Enkvist's shop in Santa Gertrudis, a 'talent scout for wines' since 2017: the island's own bottles, gin, beer, olive oil and hierbas next to serious finds from Italy, France and Austria. A tasting room for up to twenty — book a private tasting or a pop-up dinner for a winter night with friends.

All year · Tue–Sat 17:00–23:00 · Sun: Sunday Roast 13:00–17:00, then 18:00–23:00 · closed Mon

La Chickeria

Eat · Drink

The rotisserie in Santa Gertrudis (Venda de Can Llàtzer 19): organic chicken marinated in thirty-odd herbs and spices and turned over the fire, whole or half, plus chicken burgers, satay and a few vegetarian plates — eat in or take away. From this autumn Sundays are a proper British roast with Wild Health, 13:00–17:00, with a DJ and Bloody Marys. WhatsApp +34 654 04 18 00.

ERIDE Ibiza — electric retro motorcycles

Explore · Sunset

A small independent outfit in Siesta renting retro-styled electric scramblers — real motorcycles, not scooters, silent and emission-free, up to 150 km on a charge. Golden Hour Ride (€85, 1.5–2 h): a guided loop timed to the best light of the day. Coast & Curves (€160, 3.5–4 h): the coast and the inland roads of the north with lunch and a drink. A car licence (B) with five years' experience is enough in Spain; any motorcycle licence works too.
