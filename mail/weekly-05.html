A fireplace, a race and rice

Hola,

The evenings have turned, so this is the weekend the island moves indoors and around the fire: dinner by the fireplace at Las Dalias, a club turning five, a bottle of hierbas you make yourself. Still outside: a sobrassada race, the Sant Joan market's winter party with five bands, and the world championship of wild-mushroom rice. Next week the Christmas lights go on.

Tonight
Chimenea Sessions × La Tipi, Las Dalias Café, Sant Carles · 20:00
Las Dalias' winter Fridays by the fireplace: dinner first, then dancing, tonight with La Tipi bringing Words of Niō to the decks. Book a table for dinner. Details →
Also: Nabil live at Sa Carboneria in Santa Eulària (acoustic, 20:00–22:00, 2-for-1 cocktails from 19:00), little gnome houses for kids aged 6–8 at the Sant Jordi library (17:30, free, ten places), steak night at Higo in Santa Eulària, and Restaurat's €26 three-course menus at 13 restaurants in and around Sant Antoni, until 8 December.
Saturday
Make your own hierbas with Ibiza Outdoors, Sant Joan · 10:00 ★ Local
A gentle two-hour walk foraging rosemary, fennel and thyme, then an hour at Toby's place making your own bottle of Hierbas Ibicencas. It matures in three months, so it's ready for Christmas. €35, bottle included. Details →
XVIII Cursa de sa Sobrassada, Santa Gertrudis · 16:30 ★ Local
The sobrassada race through the village, part of the fiesta month. Register at sportmaniacs.com, or just stand on the square and cheer. Details →
Disco Bingo at Higo, Santa Eulària · 19:00–22:00
Bingo to disco classics during a shared dinner by Ed, with DJ Roel, and Dutch sing-alongs to finish. Shared menu €59.50, from beef tataki to chateaubriand. Book a table. Details →
Akasha turns five, Las Dalias, Sant Carles · from 20:00
Token Brothers warm up in the café, then Kerala Dust (DJ), Luca Saporito and Igor Marijuan b2b Cris 44 in the club. Ticketed. Details →
Also: the Collaborative Artistic Lab at Can Cristòfol on the Sant Carles road (10:30, booking required), Las Dalias Saturday market, Forada market, and the Sant Jordi flea market until 15:00.
Sunday
World championship of arròs amb pebrassos i trossos, Santa Gertrudis · 12:00 ★ Local
Teams cook the island's autumn rice of wild mushrooms and meat on the park square, with music. Communal rice lunch at 14:00 (€15), Bingo Trudis at 16:00. Details →
Winter kick-off at the Sant Joan market · 12:00–19:00 ★ Local
The Sunday market's start-of-winter party: five bands from 12:30 (Tukdam Project, Another Band, Beta Simon, Paraíso Calero, Yellow Fingers at 17:30), a street parade at 14:00 and batucada at 15:45. Details →
Crystal Coast posidonia walk from Portinatx · 10:00 ★ Local
Ibiza Outdoors walks the north coast for Ibiza & Formentera Preservation. Free to join, donate on the day. Details →
Also: Tapas and Tunes at Lulu on Port des Torrent beach from 13:00, and Sunday roast at La Chickeria in Santa Gertrudis, 13:00–17:00.
Next week

Monday 23 is hutspot night at Higo, with pork steak. Tuesday 24 Ibiza Outdoors walks Cala Martina under the full moon from 16:15. Wednesday 25 they are at Cala Comte for the Ibiza Henge, the rave cave and Es Vedrà.

Thursday 26 a luxury yard sale in La Chickeria's garden, 10:00–15:00. Friday 27 Sant Antoni switches on its Christmas lights and opens the chalets on Passeig de ses Fonts (18:30, date to be confirmed). Saturday 28 is a double: the Fira des Poll Pagès in Sant Miquel, a whole village cooking the island's free-range chicken from noon, and at 18:30 Ibiza Town's lights and the Vara de Rey market open with videomapping on the Passeig.
