The weekly · No. 6 · Friday 27 November

Lights on, a chicken fair and a Sunday dance

Hola,

The huts are already standing on Vara de Rey and the cables are strung across the plane trees — this is the weekend the island switches on. Sant Antoni goes first tonight, Ibiza Town follows tomorrow. In between: Sant Miquel cooks its own chicken for the whole village, Cala Llonga starts dancing salsa, and Sunday has a walk with Es Vedrà in view and a dance in the Las Dalias garden. Next week the rest of the villages light up and the Christmas markets open for real.

Tonight
Sant Antoni lights switch-on & Christmas market opening, Passeig de ses Fonts · 18:30
The first of the island's switch-ons: the lights, the market huts along the promenade, music and the children's programme. Free. Details →
ReparaLab, Fundació Deixalles, Sant Antoni · 17:30
Bring the chair with the loose leg or the lamp that stopped working: IbizaPreservation and Deixalles help you fix it instead of binning it. Free, tools provided. Details →
Also: Acoustic Fridays at Sa Carboneria in Santa Eulària from 20:00, steak night at Higo, Vrijmibo drinks at Amigos in Cala Llonga, and Restaurat's €26 menus in Sant Antoni until 8 December.
Saturday
II Fira des Poll Pagès, Sant Miquel · 12:00–20:00 ★ Local
The village cooks its own free-range chicken: families and restaurants each bring a dish and sell it by the plate, nothing over €10, jury tasting at 14:00. Last year's first edition sold 1,600 plates, so go for lunch rather than dinner. Details →
Ibiza Town: lights switch-on & Vara de Rey market opens · 18:30 ★ Local
The big one — Vara de Rey, the tent, the market huts and Parc Reina Sofía all lit at once, then open every day until Reyes. Go on foot from the port and finish with a vermut at the market. Details →
First Salsa Saturday at Amigos, Cala Llonga · 16:30 ★ Local
Cuban salsa with Orbe Yorgenis, beginners welcome, drinks after. €22 a class or ten for €188. Details →
Also: Las Dalias Saturday market in Sant Carles and the Sant Jordi flea market at the hipódromo, both from the morning.
Sunday
The Chicken Loop — Can Ramon de sa Serra & Es Vedrà views, Ibiza Outdoors · 10:00 ★ Local
A loop through the fincas behind Port des Torrent with Es Vedrà on the horizon for the second half. Donation €15–25, dogs welcome, meet at the Es Cuco car park. Details →
Nido × Visionari — Sunday dance in the Las Dalias garden, Sant Carles · from 12:00 ★ Local
The north's Sunday ritual: organic house in the garden next to the market, free until 20:00, then indoors at Akasha until late. Every Sunday until 27 December. Details →
Also: Sunday Roast at La Chickeria × Wild Health in Santa Gertrudis, roast in the garden at Can Mimosa, and the Sant Joan market from 10:00.
Next week
Stamppot Monday at Higo, Santa Eulària · Mon 30 · from 18:00 ★ Local
This week's stamppot: Ibiza-style lettuce mash with a Dutch meatball. Also takeaway; book. Details →
Wednesday walk: Dolphin Lookout above Cala d'Albarca · Wed 2 · 10:00 ★ Local
Ibiza Outdoors heads to the north-west cliffs — the quiet side of the island, with a lookout where dolphins do turn up. Donation €15–25. Details →
Santa Eulària & Sant Josep switch on · Fri 4 · 18:00
Two more villages light up on the same evening: Santa Eulària opens its market on the Passeig de s'Alamera, Sant Josep lights the church square. Pick one, or do both — they're 25 minutes apart. Details →
Press the season's flowers into a 2027 calendar, Sa Terra at Pantastic, Ibiza Town · Fri 4 · 17:00
A slow evening of pressed flowers and paper, and you leave with a calendar for the year. €55, materials included. Details →
Las Dalias Christmas Market — first weekend · Sat 5 & Sun 6
The market goes festive for the long weekend: stalls, food, music and the fire going from 10:00. Details →
The Little Farmers Fair, Juntos Farm · Sat 5 & Sun 6 · 09:30–17:00
Hands in the soil: a family weekend on the farm with workshops, animals, food and music. €25 (the first release sold out), tickets via @thelittlefarmersfair. Details →
Also over the Puente: Christmas centrepieces & candles at Sant Joan town hall (Sat 5, 16:00, €10), Eivicadabra magic shows in the Vara de Rey tent, Cañas 'n' Roll & Burger Fest in Sant Josep (Sat 5, 19:00), the Punta Galera walk (Sun 6, 10:00) and La Movida street party at the Sant Antoni market (Sun 6, 16:00).
