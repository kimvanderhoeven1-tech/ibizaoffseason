The weekly · No. 4 · Friday 13 November

A village in full swing

Hola,

Twenty-two degrees and no rain in sight until Tuesday. Santa Gertrudis is in its fiesta month, so this weekend the island's wines are poured on the square and on Monday the village has its big day. Elsewhere: paellas cooked live in Es Cubells, a trail race to Sant Carles, flamenco in the old theatre and the first winter party. Next week: hierbas, a sobrassada race and the world championship of mushroom rice.

Tonight
Flamenco Sin Fin at Teatro Pereyra, Ibiza Town · 21:30
Sevillian cantaor Ismael de la Rosa 'El Bola' with Josemi Carmona of Ketama on guitar, singer Alana Sinkëy and two dancers, seated at tables in the 1898 theatre. Doors 20:30, from €26. Details →
Pocomucho Winter Party at Paradise Lost, Ibiza Town · 19:30
The first of four winter parties: Ainsley May, BENDMZ, Louis Ryder, M.A.R.J.O.Y.A.S and Kevin B until 03:00. No VIP, no guestlist. Details →
Also: steak night at Higo in Santa Eulària, Chimenea around the fireplace at Las Dalias Café from 20:00, and paint-your-own ceramics at Studiø Raffo in Cala Llonga, 14:00–17:00.
Saturday
EiViFest, the island's wine fair in Santa Gertrudis · 13:00 to midnight ★ Local
Ibiza's own wines by the glass on the park square, food stalls in the car park next door. Vermut a 45 RPM spinning singles, Miss Indispensables live at 19:00 and DJ Toni Torres from 21:00. Kids' workshops on the same square from 10:00. Details →
The paella contest in Es Cubells · from 12:30 ★ Local
Teams cook live in the middle of the village, the jury tastes at 14:00, and there are bands and DJs into the night. Free to watch; buy a ticket on the spot for a plate. Details →
Choirs of the northern parishes, church of Sant Joan · 19:00 ★ Local
The second of the island's choir gatherings this month, in the village church. Free. Details →
Also: a day of talks on culture, land and art at Can Cristòfol on the Sant Carles road (10:30, book via WhatsApp +34 638 497 901), Las Dalias Saturday market, and the flea market in Sant Jordi until 15:00.
Sunday
XI Sant Carles Trail · 10:45 ★ Local
12 km from Cala Boix or 7 km from Pou des Lleó, both finishing in the village. Registration closed on Thursday, but the finish line in Sant Carles is a good place to be around noon, and the bars are open. Details →
Quatre estampes eivissenques, street theatre through Dalt Vila · 11:00 ★ Local
Anna Xantal's company plays four scenes from Ibiza's history and village life, moving from spot to spot through the walled town. In Catalan and Spanish, very visual. Free, no booking: be at Portal de ses Taules at 11:00 and follow the actors. Details →
Eivissa Clàssica: Basso + Basso, Sala Capitular, Dalt Vila · 12:00
Bach, Vivaldi and Purcell on violone and archlute, in the chapter room of the old town hall. Tickets per concert at eivissaclassica.com. Details →
Tapas and Tunes at Lulu, Port des Torrent · from 13:00
New on the west coast: homemade tapas, warming soups, mulled wine and DJ Jay Jay on the beach, every Sunday through the winter. Details →
Closing concert of the choir gathering, El Convent, Dalt Vila · 20:00 ★ Local
Fifty years of Amics de sa Música, celebrated in the church of Santo Domingo. Free. Details →
Also: a fruit-tree walk around Sant Joan with Ibiza Outdoors at 10:00 (free, donation for Ibiza & Formentera Preservation), Sunday roast at La Chickeria in Santa Gertrudis, and the Sant Joan market until 16:00.
Next week

Monday 16 is Santa Gertrudis day: solemn mass at noon, the procession and ball pagès on the square, and in the evening a monologue by Sr. Corrales. It's also kale stamppot night at Higo. Wednesday 18 Ibiza Outdoors walks the caves and rock pools of Xarraca, wild swim included.

Saturday 21 is a full one: pick wild herbs and make your own hierbas with Ibiza Outdoors in Sant Joan (€35, bottle included), the Cursa de sa Sobrassada in Santa Gertrudis at 16:30, and Disco Bingo with DJ Roel at Higo in the evening. Sunday 22 the world championship of arròs amb pebrassos i trossos on the square in Santa Gertrudis, with a communal rice lunch at 14:00 (€15), and a posidonia walk along the Crystal Coast from Portinatx at 10:00.

Nature of Ibiza
Slow down for the hedgehogs
November on the campo roads: this summer's young hedgehogs are out every night, eating to get heavy enough for winter, and crossing the roads to do it. They curl up instead of running, so take the bends gently after dark. Found one hurt? Call Ibanat or Can Hog Ibiza, the island's hedgehog rescue.
