Wellness

The island slows down. So can you.

Winter is when the island actually does the things it advertises in summer: a reformer class at nine with eight people, a sauna with nobody in it, a sea you have to yourself at sunrise. The big hotel spas close in October; this is what stays open — drop-in classes, the spas and saunas with winter hours, cold water, and the one-day retreats and circles. Every entry has a Book button that goes straight to the studio. For week-long retreats, see Retreats; sports and group runs are on the Agenda.

Closed for winter (so you don't drive there): Six Senses, Nobu, Open Spa at Ibiza Gran Hotel, 7Pines, BLESS, Na Xamena, Aguas de Ibiza, Atzaró — all shut by the end of October, back around April. Teach a class or run a circle? Send it in and it goes on here and in the weekly.
