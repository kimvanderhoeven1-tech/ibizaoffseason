Lights off, lanterns on

Hola,

The clocks go back on Saturday night, so the island gets an extra hour of Halloween and loses an hour of sunset. Then Sunday is Tots Sants, bunyols and cemeteries full of flowers, and on Wednesday Sant Carles has its day. The villages are open; the clubs are not. That's the deal from here on.

Tonight
Friday tardeo at Atelier MosaArtBySteph, Santa Eulària · 16:00–20:00 ★ Local
Steph opens her mosaic atelier for the end-of-season sale: bowls, mirrors, geckos, lamps, all handmade, with a cold drink in hand. Free.
Horror double bill: Weapons & The Monkey · 20:00
Two original-version horror films, one at Can Jeroni in Sant Josep, one at the Caló de s'Oli auditorium in Cala de Bou. Free. Start times vary between 20:00 and 21:30 — check before you go.
Cinefòrum: Gattaca at the UIB · 18:30 ★ Local
The university's free film forum in Ibiza Town: the film, then the debate. Free.
Also: the Ibiza Halloween Festival at Can Ventosa runs through Sunday (films, FX make-up workshops, the Terror Express phone-film contest, free), and Studiø Raffo in Cala Llonga has a paint-your-own-ceramics afternoon from 14:00 (€79).
Saturday · Halloween
Monster make-up and the Monster Walk, Vara de Rey · 17:00
Free monster make-up from five, then the costume parade through town to a red carpet with prizes. The island's biggest Halloween for families. Free.
Stargazing at the Cala d'Hort observatory · 20:30 ★ Local
Telescopes under the darkest sky on the island, free bus, ages 6+. Places were by email last weekend; if you got one, this is your reminder.
Four choirs in the church of Sant Antoni · 20:00 ★ Local
The Trobada de Corals moves west: the parish choir, Cor de ses Figueretes, the Ukrainian Greek Catholic choir and more. Free, and nothing to do with Halloween, which is the point.
Halloween at Pikes · 21:00
The haunted hotel, costume compulsory, charity night, until 06:00. Pikes also hosts The Haunted Ballroom at 528 with Erol Alkan and Leon Vynehall.
Also: Coco, el musical at the Recinto Ferial at 18:00 (ages 3+, €8/€12, again Sunday at noon), the 14 km coastal walk from Sant Carles church at 10:00 (free, register turisme@santaeularia.com), and it is the last day to enrol for Santa Eulària's free Spanish classes: passport and padrón to the town hall.
Sunday · Tots Sants
Children's fiesta and trencada, Sant Rafel · from 10:00 ★ Local
Nut-cracking, workshops and bouncy castles in the marquee, lunch at the football ground, and the Trencada at 20:00 to close the village fiestas. Free.
Nido x Día de Muertos, Las Dalias garden · 12:00
Day of the Dead edition of the Sunday garden session; free in the garden until 20:00, then the club from €10.
Theatrical visit to the old cemetery, Ibiza Town · 18:00 ★ Local
Actors tell the stories of the Cementeri Vell on All Saints' night. Check times with the council.
Also: the Podenc Race in Santa Gertrudis at 10:00 (pairs, ten obstacles, named after the island's hunting dog) opens the village's fiesta month. And eat a bunyol: every bakery fries them today.
Next week

Wednesday 4 is Sant Carles. Mass and procession at midday, ball pagès, a hippy market in the afternoon, music into the night, free food in the square — the north's best fiesta, with Las Dalias next door. Go.

Thursday 5 the Festival del Humor opens at Can Ventosa (four comedy nights, in Spanish, €20). Saturday 7: the council's free heritage walk through Dalt Vila at 10:30, Sant Carles Xtrem obstacle race at Las Dalias (kids free), and at 18:00 the opening of Co-Creating Culture at Can Cristòfol on the Sant Carles road, an exhibition curated by Roseline de Thélin, with a speakers' day on the 14th and a collaborative art lab on the 21st (book via WhatsApp Bex, +34 638 497 901). Sunday 8: choirs in the church of Sant Josep at noon, vermouth on the square after.

The coast path from Sant Carles
