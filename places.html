Open in Winter

"What's still open in winter?"

It's the question everyone asks from November on: where can you still eat, drink, dance and sit in the sun? Here's our answer — the restaurants, bars, fincas, markets, galleries and little Spanish village spots that keep going all winter, with a terrace in the sun, a fondue, a concert, an opening or a sunset. Winter is the art season here — the galleries that close in August are the ones that open in November; tap Art to see them. And the things no guide lists — live music in a Spanish café, a bingo night, a village bar with one dish — carry a Local tag. Tap Breakfast for where the island starts its day.
