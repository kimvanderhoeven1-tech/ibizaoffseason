Dance it out

Hola,

First one. From now on, every Friday: what's actually on, picked by someone who lives here year-round.

This is the switch-over weekend. The summer places play their last records, and the villages are only just getting started. Go to both.

Tonight
La Tipi at Akasha, Las Dalias · 23:00
Friday in the club under the trees: Tomy Velez, Lumio, Words of Nio, Sarcasmo, David Osorio. From €15.
Flash Closing at Pikes · 21:00
DiY Soundsystem x Music for Freaks and friends, hosted by Lucy Fizz. First of three closing nights.
Sant Rafel warms up · from the evening
Night walk, sobrassada on the grill, Arredefolk in the marquee. Hot chocolate and bunyols for the kids. Free.
Saturday
Decorative ceramics painting, Can Ros, Santa Eulària · 11:00
Paint traditional Ibizan ceramics with Celia Casanova in the ethnographic museum on Puig de Missa. Free, register visitapatrimoni@conselldeivissa.es. Ages 16+.
One Last Summer Dance, Dalt Vila · from 14:00
House and disco on two stages under the walls, Ibiza Spotlight's 50th birthday. Free, open air, all afternoon.
Festes de Sant Rafel — the big day
Mass and procession, ball pagès by Sa Colla, tasting of island products, then concerts into the night. The real thing. Free.
Pikes House Party Closing · 21:00
Groove Armada and Bushwacka! close the hotel's 2026 season, with El Conchitas, Lilli Kelly, Andy Baxter and Quantum Disco. One more big one before spring.
Also: Supernova in the Las Dalias garden from 16:00 (free until 22:00), Palestrina sung inside the golden Cel d'Or room at MACE at 19:00 (free), and the Trail Marathon out of Sant Antoni from 07:30 — go and cheer.
Sunday
Festa Pagesa, Santa Gertrudis ★ Local
The old island on the square: dancing, crafts, folk games, pagès food. Free, all day.
Nido Summer Closing, Las Dalias garden · 16:00
The last garden session of the year, free until 23:00.
Cala Escondida closing party · 18:30
The eco beach bar at the quiet end of Platja d'en Bossa closes with Soul Doctor live. Free.
Sundays at Pikes — the closing · 21:00
D'Julz, Alessio Collino, Neil Diablo and the Sundays crew. Then the doors shut until spring.
Also: the flea market on the church square in Jesús (10:30) and the autumn stock fair on Vara de Rey.
Next week

Halloween is Saturday 31. Families: free monster make-up on Vara de Rey from 17:00, then the Monster Walk through town. Grown-ups: Pikes, costume compulsory, until 06:00. The quiet ones: a stargazing night at the Cala d'Hort observatory, 20:30, free bus — places by email this weekend only.

Wednesday 28 is the last Punta Arabí hippy market of the year. Thursday 29 the Ibiza Halloween Festival opens at Can Ventosa, four days, free. Sunday 1 November is Tots Sants: kids' fiesta in Sant Rafel, Día de Muertos at Las Dalias, and a theatrical night tour of the old cemetery in town.
